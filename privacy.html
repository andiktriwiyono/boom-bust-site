Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

This policy describes how the “Boom & Bust” Google OAuth application (“the App”) handles data. The App is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of the Boom & Bust YouTube channel to read the channel’s own analytics and manage the channel’s own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the App accesses

With the channel owner’s consent, the App accesses the following through the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API:

  • Channel and video information (youtube.readonly) — read-only access to the channel’s own videos, playlists, and account details.
  • Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) — read-only views, watch time, and audience reports for the channel’s own content.
  • Monetary analytics reports (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly) — read-only revenue reports for the channel’s own content.
  • Video management (youtube.force-ssl and youtube.upload) — viewing, editing, and uploading the channel’s own videos, and managing their titles, descriptions, and comments.

How the data is used

Access is used only to display the channel’s own analytics to its owner and to help the owner manage the channel’s own videos (for example, updating a title or description). The App does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the channel.

Where the data is stored

Authorization tokens are stored only on the channel owner’s own computer, in a local credentials file. Analytics results are processed on that same computer. No channel data is stored on a server operated by the App, and no data is sent to any third party other than Google’s own APIs, which the App calls on the owner’s behalf.

What is never done with the data

  • The data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • The data is never used to identify or contact any viewer.
  • No viewer or subscriber data is collected, stored, or processed by the App.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the App’s access at any time from their Google account’s third-party access settings. Revoking access immediately stops the App from reading or managing channel data, and the locally stored tokens can be deleted from the owner’s computer.
